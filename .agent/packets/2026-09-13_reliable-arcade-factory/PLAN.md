## Summary
Finish a reliable local Three.js/NexusEngine factory and usable arcade containing 1,000 visually and behaviorally distinct accepted games. Implementation is now authorized and underway. Current evidence lives in NexusArcade-Experiments/campaigns/reliable-arcade-factory/goals/G01. This does not mark any of the 33 goals complete.

Full requirements are in RULES.md; every goal has a linked specification and a queue entry with separate specificIntent and preparedInterpretation objects.
